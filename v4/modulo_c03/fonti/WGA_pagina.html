This is a standard 24 volt brushed DC gear motor, generating 30kg.cm of torque at 12rpm. It's a small, inexpensive motor suitable for low torque, high speed applications. Combined with a 422:1 worm gearbox, this motor will provide greater efficiency, higher torque, and quieter motor operation.

Electrical Specification

  • Part Number: WGA-4058247000-G422
  • Rated Voltage: 24V
  • Rated Torque: 30kg.cm(416.62oz.in)
  • Rated Current: <=1500mA
  • Rated Speed: 12rpm
  • No-load Current: <=550mA
  • No-load Speed: 16rpm
  • Rotation Direction: CW/CCW
  • Motor Type: Brushed DC Motor

Gearbox Specification

  • Gearbox Type: worm gearbox
  • Gear Ratio: 422:1

  • Gearbox Size: 40 x 58 x 26.5mm
